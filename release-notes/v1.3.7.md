## Fixes
- **No more gaps inside words when pinyin is hidden.** Some words, most noticeably 船长 (captain), had extra space between and around their characters even with pinyin turned off. This happened whenever a word's pinyin was wider than its characters. Hidden pinyin now takes up no space, so every word is spaced normally. About 200 words were affected, including 上上下下, 常常, 想想 and 商店. (#27, #29)
- Turning on pinyin (拼) for a bubble now shows it instantly instead of fading it in.

## Testing status
- The spacing fix was checked in a test browser before release.
- Levels 1–3 have been checked more closely for visuals. The final levels will get the same check as they become available. (#20)
- Native speaker review of the dialogue is still pending. (#4)
