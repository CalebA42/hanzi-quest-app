## Fixes
- **The blue flower no longer disappears into the sky.** In level 4 the flower's petals were the same blue as the sky behind them. They now have their own deeper blue and a dark outline. (#20, #35)
- **You can now see the seeds the snake gives you.** If you make peace with the snake in level 4, the seeds appear on screen as it hands them over, and the ending shows a new sprout grown from them instead of a full flower. (#20, #35)
- **The captain now stands in the boat.** At sea in level 4, the captain used to bob up and down in front of the boat, out of step with it. The captain now stands on deck and rocks with the boat. (#20, #35)
- **The yeti's chest no longer has see-through spots.** Two missing pixels on the yeti in level 5 looked like dots on its chest and let the background show through. (#20, #35)

## Testing status
- The sprite fixes were checked as drawings on the scenes they appear in, but haven't yet been played through in the app or on a phone. (#35)
- Levels 4–6 have now had the same visual check as levels 1–3, so every level has been checked. Other sprite issues listed in #20 are still open.
- Native speaker review of the dialogue is still pending. (#4)
