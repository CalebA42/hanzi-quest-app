## New
- **All-new 16-bit pixel art.** Every scene and character in all six levels has been redrawn in a richer pixel-art style, with one shared colour palette, bold outlines, light and shadow, and far more detail than before. Text, menus, buttons and your saved games are unchanged. (#34)
- **Scenes come alive.** Torches flicker, fireflies drift through the forest, rivers flow, snow falls in the mountain village, waves roll at the harbour and the boat rocks in the storm. Animations are slow and gentle, and they stop if your device is set to reduce motion. (#34)
- **Characters move and react.** Everyone has a calm idle animation (blinking, talking, a cat's tail, a snake's tongue), and enemies flinch with a flash and a short knock-back when you hit them in a fight. (#34)
- **Bigger, more detailed enemies in fights.** The rat, dog, wolf, thief, snake, yeti and stone golem each have larger fight art, with a hurt pose when they take a hit. (#34)
- **A calm sea for the trip home.** After the island in level 4, you sail home under clearing skies and a faint rainbow instead of back through the storm. (#34)
- **The yeti looks happy in the village.** In the friendly ending of level 5, the yeti smiles and waves instead of crying. (#34)

## Fixes
- **Animations run at the same speed on every screen.** On high-refresh-rate displays, scene effects and the flash and shake when someone is hit used to play faster than intended. They're now timed by the clock, not the screen. (#34)

## Testing status
- Every scene was checked as a picture against its story node, and every level's main scenes were opened in Firefox without errors. The new art hasn't yet been played through from start to finish, tried on a phone, or tested with reduce motion turned on. (#34)
- The AppImage and Windows builds of this version haven't been tested yet.
- The sprite problems listed in #20 came from the old art, which this version replaces. They'll need a fresh look against the new art before #20 is closed.
- Native speaker review of the dialogue is still pending. (#4)
